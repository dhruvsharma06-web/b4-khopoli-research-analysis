Marketing strategy

The house lists in April 2027. Three months of build-up first, so the first peak season is full.

“Your own hill home, 2 km from Imagicaa.” Arrive, pool at sunset, dinner on the terrace.

3 posts a week: rooms, terrace pool, nearby spots. Collabs with Mumbai & Pune travel creators.

Approach

A KDD process: data → knowledge → decision.

01

Collect

226 villa listings and 39 rentals from brands, Airbnb and rent portals.

02

Clean

Removed duplicates and outliers, filled gaps → 192 villas, 35 rentals.

03

Warehouse

One SQLite star schema: 8 dimension tables, 3 fact tables.

04

Select

Kept what drives price: size, brand tier, location, price type.

05

Transform

Log prices, brand tiers, flags for “from”, tax and weekend prices.

06

Train

Ridge for nightly price (±24% error). Random Forest for rent.

07

Evaluate risk

10,000 simulated years per option: bad year, good year, chance of loss.

08

Calibrate

Real booking data from 770+ market listings. 42 rentals for rent.

What I learned

Size beats everything

Big villas earn 3× more per night than 2BHKs.

Brand matters

Premium brands charge ~26% more for the same size.

Weekends sell

Weekends book ~2.3× more than weekdays.

Limits

No Khopoli-only data

Lonavala and Karjat are used as the market.

Brand terms assumed

Real quotes from brands will sharpen the numbers.

Every house is different

Photos, reviews and views move results up or down.
